# Room pictures

Put the 4 room pictures here. The page picks one by the time on your computer:

| File | Shown |
|---|---|
| `room-morning.webp` | 6am to 11am |
| `room-afternoon.webp` | 11am to 5pm |
| `room-evening.webp` | 5pm to 8pm |
| `room-night.webp` | 8pm to 6am |

Until a picture exists, the page shows a plain sky gradient for that time instead.

Make them 16:9, at least 1920x1080, and save as WebP (or tell Claude to convert PNGs). Generate the morning one first, then use the AI tool's
**edit** feature on that same picture for the other three, so the furniture stays in the same place
(the clickable spots on the page depend on that).

These pictures are made by us with an AI tool. The reference pictures used for the idea
belong to other people and are not used in the app.

## 1. Morning (generate this first)

Wide 16:9 anime film background illustration of a cozy student study bedroom, seen from eye level
with a slightly wide angle. Painterly, hand-painted look with soft brush texture, rich small details,
cinematic lighting, gentle light rays and floating dust, warm nostalgic everyday atmosphere.
Layout: a large window on the right half showing a quiet residential town and the sky; a wooden
study desk under the window across the lower right, with a clear empty spot in the middle of the
desktop; a bendable desk lamp at the left end of the desk; a small drawer unit under the desk; a
round wall clock and a paper wall calendar on the wall left of the window; a tall bookshelf with
textbooks and binders on the far left; a few potted plants, sticky notes, stacked books and a mug.
Keep the left third calmer, with a mostly plain wall. No people, no animals, no text, no logos.
Time: morning, soft cool sunlight through the window, fresh blue sky with light clouds.

## 2. Afternoon (edit picture 1)

Same room, same camera angle, every object in exactly the same place. Change only the time of day
to late afternoon: warm golden sunlight streaming in at a low angle, long shadows, slightly orange sky.

## 3. Evening (edit picture 1)

Same room, same camera, everything in the same place. Change only the time to sunset: orange and
pink sky with purple clouds, the room dimmer, and the desk lamp switched on with a warm glow.

## 4. Night (edit picture 1)

Same room, same camera, everything in the same place. Change only the time to night: deep blue sky
with stars and a few lit windows in the town, the room dark and moody, lit mainly by the warm desk
lamp, soft moonlight on the window frame.
